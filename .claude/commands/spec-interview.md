Do NOT write any code or create any files until Step 4.

## Step 1 — Interview (one question at a time)
1. Describe the app. Who uses it, what problem does it solve?
2. What are the 3–5 features that MUST be in v1?
3. Hard constraints? (auth, real-time, mobile, offline, specific stack?)
4. What does successful v1 look like for users?

## Step 2 — Draft PRD
Sections: Problem, Users, MVP features (numbered with acceptance criteria),
Out of scope for v1, Constraints, Success criteria.

## Step 3 — Scope check
Flag non-MVP items. Ask keep or cut before saving.

## Step 4 — Save
Write to docs/PRD.md. Print line count. Stop.
